Featured Audit Case Study Laguna Zone • Annual Physical Inventory
SAP S/4HANA vs. Physical Custody

Personnel Accountability Variance Detection & Escalation

During the Annual Physical Inventory audit, physical-to-digital verification uncovered a custody discrepancy between SAP S/4HANA accountability records and actual on-hand telecommunications equipment.

• Finding: Digital records across the entire Laguna zone showed a total of 71,719 units of telecommunications equipment scheduled for audit, while physical inspection confirmed 69,667 units on hand.

• Action Taken: Documented the exact 2,052 unit negative variance with supporting audit logs and formally escalated the findings to the personnel's supervisor.

• Outcome: Initiated an official internal office memo requiring formal explanation of the missing equipment and restoring accurate accountability records.

Variance Reconciliation Summary

71,719 SAP Digital Record
69,667 Physical Count
-2,052 Escalated Variance
Control Impact: Prevented unrecorded asset loss and enforced supervisory accountability through formal memo documentation.
